About BYAR ONLINE STORE

A neighborhood general store, now online.

BYAR ONLINE STORE started as a local general store and now takes orders online. The idea is simple: browse what you need, send your list, and get it by pickup or delivery — no app to install, no account to create.

What we sell

Everyday grocery and household essentials, personal care items, stationery, and a growing range of computer accessories, headphones and earbuds — all in one list.

How ordering works

Add items to your list on the site, choose pickup or delivery, pick a payment method, and send the order — it goes straight to us on WhatsApp, and you'll get a reply to confirm.

How you can pay

Cash on pickup or delivery, JazzCash, Easypaisa, or bank transfer. For JazzCash and Easypaisa, we ask for the transaction ID so we can confirm your payment quickly.
