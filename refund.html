Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026

Groceries and household items

Because these are everyday consumables, we can't accept returns once delivered — except if you received the wrong item, a damaged item, or something past its expiry date. Message us on WhatsApp the same day with a photo, and we'll replace it or refund that item.

Computer accessories, headphones & earbuds

If an item arrives faulty or dead-on-arrival, let us know within 3 days of delivery. We'll ask for the packaging to still be intact and the item unused. We'll then replace it or refund it after a quick check.

We don't offer refunds on electronics for a simple change of mind once the packaging has been opened.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back the same way you paid — cash refunds in person, or a JazzCash/Easypaisa/bank transfer to the same account, usually within a few days of us confirming the return.

Questions about an order

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and we'll sort it out.
